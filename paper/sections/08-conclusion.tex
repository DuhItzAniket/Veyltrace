\section{Conclusion and Data Availability}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built \sys on the rule that AI proposes and code decides, and tested it against hypotheses frozen before the first test run; the deviations from that plan, five of them made or logged after results were seen, are listed in docs/04 §12 and reported where they apply.
On RCAEval RE2 with the oracle alert, the deterministic pipeline almost always retrieves the true root but loses it in re-ranking, and it is worse in AC@1 than N-Sigma on every system and pooled (pooled difference \resnum{re2-oracle-rq1-v1}{pooled/veyltrace:off-vs-best_simple_baseline/ac_at_1/difference}), with each 95\% interval below 0 but a sign-flip test that is not significant on TT (Holm-adjusted $p$ \resnum{re2-oracle-rq1-v1}{tt/veyltrace:off-vs-best_simple_baseline/ac_at_1/holm_p}).
Code-verified proposals from a local model neither significantly lower nor detectably raise accuracy (pooled lift \resnum{re2-oracle-rq2-v1}{pooled/veyltrace:brief-vs-veyltrace:off/ac_at_1/difference}); verification keeps the verdict independent of proposal order, and a planted decoy placed first becomes the top-1 of an agent that trusts the model's order by construction, while it changes the verified top-1 far less often (an exploratory comparison with no test), though still above the 0.05 target on OB and SS.
The AI-off candidate sets reach the 0.90 coverage target within RE2 (\resnum{rq3-intervals/re2-oracle-rq3-off-v1}{intervals.pooled.coverage.estimate}) but not when TT is held out (\resnum{re2-oracle-loso-off-v1}{tt/veyltrace:off/coverage}), they are larger than three candidates on two systems, and, as fitted under cross-fitting on RE2, the no-fault answer never fires on held-out normal windows.
The pipeline tolerates alert shifts on OB and SS but not on TT, resists synthetic decoy changes, exceeds its time budgets on TT and for the proposer, and replays every reproduced verdict to the same hash, though Tier-1 replay falls just short of 100\%.
The verifier's design goals, auditability and resistance to injected claims, are met in part; its accuracy is not, and the re-ranking step is where the next version must improve.

\paragraph{Data availability}
Code, experiment configurations, every summary and every per-case output (\code{per_case.csv}) are in the archived artifact; the AI arms' recordings (verdicts, slates, cassettes) contain benchmark telemetry and are shared privately on request (REPRODUCE.md §3).
The artifact is the Zenodo archive of release v1.0.0 (the DOI is minted when the release is published and then added to \code{CITATION.cff} and \code{README.md} on \code{main}); \code{uv run veyltrace bench tables --check} regenerates every table and figure from the committed results and checks it against the committed file (REPRODUCE.md §1).
Raw benchmark telemetry is not redistributed; its licences are respected.
