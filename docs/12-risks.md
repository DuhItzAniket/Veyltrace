# 12 · Risk register

> **Status:** v1.0-draft · **Owner:** Aniket Kumar (lead) · **Last updated:** 2026-10-30
> Reviewed at every Sunday milestone review ([08 §7](08-collaboration-and-git.md)). The lead updates likelihood, impact and status; owners report triggers in the daily standup. A risk that fires becomes a GitHub issue labelled `risk` and, if it changes scope, an ADR line ([07 §1.1](07-roadmap.md)).
> Owners: **AK** Aniket Kumar (Reason, lead) · **AS** Annapurna S (Observe) · **CL** C Lakshanaa (Explain). Numbers are cited or labelled *estimate* / *a priori*.

## 1. Scales

| Level | Likelihood (within the month) | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **H** | More likely than not, or already showing signs | Blocks a milestone exit criterion or a "never cut" item ([07 §1.1](07-roadmap.md)), or invalidates a paper claim |
| **M** | Plausible; has happened in similar projects or is unverified on our hardware | Delays a milestone by ≤ 2 days, or forces a cut-list item, or weakens a claim |
| **L** | Unlikely given current evidence | Local rework; no milestone impact |
