# The R-16 cut list: nine experiments and one matrix cell are reported "not run"

- **Status:** Accepted (lead, #45 comment 7064502178, 2026-11-21)
- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Author:** Aniket Kumar (AK)

## Context

R-16 (#45) must fill every cell of the 04 §6 matrix from a committed config, or mark it "not run" with a reason, by M4 (Sun 29 Nov). The tables of R-17 must be generated on Sun 29, so runs end by about Sat 28, 18:00.

The R-16 plan (#45 §4) sizes the work at about 110 CPU slot-hours with upstream reuse and about 120 LLM hours. That compares with about 92 LLM hours available on one host from Tue 24 evening to Sat 28 18:00 (*estimate*). The LLM is the bottleneck. 04 §10 rule 8 says cut items appear in the matrix as "not run" with the ADR reference. 07 §1.1 says each cut gets an ADR line so the paper reports it honestly.

## Decision drivers

- Every cell the paper's tables show is either a committed number or "not run" with a printed reason (04 §10.8).
- Keep the confirmatory cells (H1–H4 at the pre-registered shifts and corners) and cut exploratory or redundant ones first.
- Stay within the LLM budget on one host.

## Considered options

1. **Cut as posted in #45 §6.** Report the 9 experiments and the Cloud-OpsBench cell as "not run".
2. **Run everything, and let late cells fail.** Unfinished cells would become "not run: compute budget" with no prior decision.
3. **Cut fewer AI cells and drop RCAEval-C AI entirely.** This loses H4c's AI arm.

## Decision

We choose **option 1**. The table maps each cut to the reason the paper prints. `bench/results/not_run.yaml` holds the same text, and `veyltrace bench tables` reads it.

| Experiment or cell | Reason the paper prints | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `fse26-oracle-rq1-v1` | stretch dataset; size, format and licence not verified | 07 §1.1 cut item 2 (FSE'26 TrainTicket adapter) |
| `re2-oracle-investigate-v1`, `re2-oracle-rq3-investigate-v1` | `investigate` mode is cut | 07 §1.1 cut item 3 (Relentra `investigate`; `brief` kept) |
| `rcaevalc-pi0-l2-ai-v1`, `rcaevalc-pi1-l2-ai-v1`, `rcaevalc-pi05-l0-ai-v1`, `rcaevalc-pi05-l2-ai-v1`, `rcaevalc-pi05-l6-ai-v1` | LLM budget; the 4 corner cells bracket the effect | **new cut, this ADR**: the AI arm keeps the corners π ∈ {0, 1} × rate ∈ {0, 6}/h, which bound the decoy effect H4c measures |
| `re2-shift-m30-brief-v1`, `re2-shift-p30-brief-v1` | ±30 s is exploratory in 04 §2; LLM budget | **new cut, this ADR**: H4a is confirmatory at ±60 and ±120 s only (04 §2) |
| `cloud-opsbench/agent-only` (04 §6 cell, no YAML) | optional in 04 §3; no experiment YAML | 04 §3 lists Cloud-OpsBench as optional |

**Run if time remains**, in this order: the Nezha AI cells, then the remaining RCAEval-C AI cells, then the ±30 s brief cells (#45 §6). A cut experiment that does run later is reported from its committed results. The generator prints results whenever its directory exists, so the register entry only applies while there are none. Remove the entry in the same PR that commits the results.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run everything, let it fail late | no decision needed | the reason would be "compute budget", decided by the clock, not by the protocol; risks confirmatory cells | dishonest ordering of what is lost |
| Drop RCAEval-C AI entirely | saves more LLM hours | loses H4c's AI arm; the corners are cheap | the corners bracket the effect at a fraction of the cost |

## Consequences

- **Positive:** every cut cell prints one reason, the same in the tables and the paper. Table A0 lists them all.
- **Negative / costs:** H4c's AI arm has no mid-grid points, and the brief shift curve has no ±30 s points.
- **Follow-ups:** if time remains, run the cuts in the order above and remove their entries. The paper's threats-to-validity section cites this ADR.

## Confirmation

`bench/tests/test_not_run_register.py` pins the register to these entries and checks that `veyltrace bench tables` prints each as "not run" with its reason.

## Addendum, 22 Nov 2026: the two VeylLab experiments

`veyllab-oracle-rq1-v1` and `veyllab-oracle-rq2-v1` are also reported "not run" for R-16, with the reason "VeylLab: 8 of 120 captures, no adapter yet (X-11, O-07); X-14 moves to v1.1 (ADR 20261121-ak-r16-cut-list)".

- **Basis:** the lab collection (X-11) has 8 kept captures against the target of ≥ 120 validated cases by M4 (07, X-11). The OTLP converter and `veyllab` adapter (O-07, #24) come after M4. Both lanes asked for these cuts on #45: Observe in comment 7064827650 (21 Nov), Explain in comment 7075062768 (22 Nov). The VeylLab dataset release (X-14) moves to v1.1 (07 §1.1 cut item 6).
- **Not in the run-if-time order:** these two cannot run before the captures and the adapter exist, whatever compute remains.
- **Consequences:** RQ1, RQ3 and RQ4 on lab data (change-aware accuracy, coverage and NO_FAULT rates on VeylLab) are not reported in v1.0. The RCAEval and RE2 cells carry those questions. The paper's threats-to-validity section cites this addendum.
- `bench/results/not_run.yaml` and `bench/tests/test_not_run_register.py` add both entries.

## Addendum, 25 Nov 2026: the remaining LLM-heavy AI arms (LLM budget)

**Decision (Aniket, lead, 25 Nov; #532).** v1.0 runs the AI arm only where it already has recordings: `re2-oracle-rq2-v1` (RE2, oracle alert), plus the two experiments that reuse those recordings without a model call. Every other AI arm is cut for v1.0 and is a candidate for v1.2. Every AI-off experiment still runs.

| Experiment | Status in v1.0 |
|---|---|
| `re2-detected-rq2-v1`, `re3-oracle-rq2-v1`, `nezha-oracle-brief-v1`, `re2-shift-{m60,p60,m120,p120}-brief-v1`, `rcaevalc-{pi0,pi1}-{l0,l6}-ai-v1` | **cut**, reported "not run" with the reason "LLM budget; v1.0 runs the AI arm only on RE2 with the oracle alert, no shift and no decoy changes; candidate for v1.2 (ADR 20261121-ak-r16-cut-list)". The five mid-grid RCAEval-C cells of 21 Nov take the same reason, because "the 4 corner cells bracket the effect" is no longer true once the corners are cut |
| `re2-oracle-rq3-v1`, `re2-oracle-loso-v1` | **revised** (04 §12): run as `re2-oracle-rq3-off-v1` and `re2-oracle-loso-off-v1`, which are the same YAMLs without the `veyltrace:brief` arm. The register maps each to its pre-registered id (`{revises: …}`), so the tables label the results "revises <id>" |
| `re2-oracle-planted-v1`, `re2-oracle-permute-v1` | **kept**: their derived runs restart from FaultLoom on the recorded brief runs, with no model call (`veyltrace_bench.planted`) |
| every AI-off experiment (detected rq1, prior-only, the six shifts, the four drops and metrics-only, the RCAEval-C AI-off grid, RE3 and Nezha rq1, the RE2→Nezha transfer) | **kept**, unchanged |

- **Basis.** A `brief` run takes about 3 min of model time on the recording laptop (R-16 rq2 recording; *estimate*). The cut arms need several thousand new model runs, roughly 200 h on that laptop (*estimate*). That is far more than the ~70 h left before compute ends on Sat 28 Nov, 18:00, and that time also has to hold the AI-off set (about 40 h of CPU, *estimate*). A rented GPU was considered (`.claude/drafts/cloud-llm-runbook.md`), and the lead declined it for v1.0.
- **Test results seen before this decision: yes.** `re2-oracle-rq1-v1` (#464) and `re2-oracle-rq2-v1` (#527) were committed before it. Table 6 showed `veyltrace:brief` within ±0.03 AC@1 of `veyltrace:off` on every RE2 system. None of the cut arms had run, so no cut result was seen. The paper states both facts. The reason is compute, and the cut is uniform: every arm that needs new model runs.
- **What v1.0 no longer reports.**
  - H2a (proposer lift) beyond RE2 with the oracle alert: no detected alert, RE3 or Nezha.
  - H4a and H4c for the AI arm: shifts and decoy changes are measured AI-off only.
  - RQ3 (coverage, set size, AURC, NO_FAULT, LOSO) for the AI path. H3a–H3d and SC-4 are estimated on the deterministic path, which is the path the conformal set and NO_FAULT are calibrated on in v1.0.
- **Unchanged.** H1a–H1c, H2a on RE2 (oracle), H2b (planted), H2c (permute), H4a–H4c AI-off, RQ5 and RQ6 (`re2-oracle-rq2-v1`'s runs).
- **v1.2.** Re-running a cut arm later needs only its pre-registered YAML at `results-code-v1` and model time. Its results would be reported as a post-v1.0 addition, with this addendum cited.
- `bench/results/not_run.yaml`, `bench/tests/test_not_run_register.py` and the 04 §12 row record the same.
